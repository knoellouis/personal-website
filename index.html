About Me

Hi, I'm Keitha Noel-Louis, a frontend developer based in Hamburg, Germany, with five years of professional experience building responsive and accessible web interfaces.

I've worked with HTML, CSS, SCSS, JavaScript and Tailwind CSS, as well as TYPO3 and WordPress. I enjoy translating design concepts into functional, user-friendly websites while maintaining clean, reusable and maintainable code.

I have experience collaborating with UX/UI designers in Figma, implementing interfaces according to WCAG 2.1 accessibility standards, and contributing to frontend best practices within a team.

I'm currently deepening my knowledge of React and TypeScript through personal projects and exploring AI-assisted tools in my development workflow.

My goal is to continue growing as a software developer while building thoughtful digital experiences that combine good design with reliable functionality.
